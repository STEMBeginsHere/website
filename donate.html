Schools pay $0. That's only possible because of you.

Every kit, every sensor, and every hour of instruction is given away. We rely on people and organizations who give money, space, equipment, or time so that no school, student, or family ever sees a bill.

Where it goes

What your gift puts in a classroom.

Hardware wears out and occasionally gets wired backwards. As donations grow, we want every student to take their kit home and keep building. Here is what your gift covers.

$25

A sensor pack

Motion, distance, moisture, and temperature sensors for one student's project.

$50

A student kit

A microcontroller, breadboard, wires, LEDs, and sensors for one student to take home and keep building with.

$100

A pair of kits

Hardware kits for two students, so a pair can build side by side.

$250

A new club

A starter set of shared kits so a new club can hold its first meetings.

Give money

Make a gift.

One-time or monthly. Monthly gifts are especially helpful because they let us say yes to schools months in advance.

  • ✓ 100% goes to kits, materials, and running workshops
  • ✓ You'll get a receipt for your records

Prefer to give by check or want to discuss a larger gift or sponsorship? Email stembeginshere@gmail.com

Gifts are processed securely by Zeffy, which charges us no fees. You'll pick your amount again on the Zeffy form.
