Media Policy

How we use photos and video of students, and how you can ask us to stop.

STEMBeginsHere occasionally photographs and films students during program activities. This page explains how we use those images and how you can ask us to stop.

What we may use images for

With the permission given by a parent or guardian at registration, we may use photos and video of your student in:

  • Our website and social media accounts
  • Videos we publish on platforms such as YouTube
  • Printed and digital promotional materials (flyers, newsletters, presentations)
  • Reports we share with funders and school partners
  • News coverage by outside media outlets, which may use the photos and video we share with them

What we do not do

  • We do not sell photos or video of any student.
  • We do not share images with third parties for advertising purposes.
  • We do not publish a student's full name alongside their photo or video.

Press, TV, and other media outlets

Outside media outlets, including newspapers and magazines, television and radio, online news sites, podcasts, and digital or social media channels run by journalists or content creators, may use photos and video we share with them for coverage of our programs. This is covered by the permission given at registration.

Interviews and names are different. If an outlet wants to interview a specific student, or to publish a student's name, we will first contact that student's parent or guardian for separate, explicit permission. Registration consent alone does not cover interviews or names.

Your choices

  • You may decline photo/video use entirely at any time, with no effect on your student's ability to participate in this or any future STEMBeginsHere program.
  • If an image of your student has already been published and you would like it removed, email us at stembeginshere@gmail.com and we will remove it within a reasonable time.
  • You may also request, at any time, to see where your student's image has been used.

Questions

Contact us at stembeginshere@gmail.com with any questions about this policy.
