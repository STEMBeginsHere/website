Local Teen-Founded Nonprofit Wraps First Free Hardware Bootcamp, Bringing Hands-On STEM Access to Phoenix-Area Students

STEMBeginsHere, founded and led by a local high schooler, gave middle and high schoolers hands-on circuit-building and coding experience — at no cost to families

PHOENIX, AZ — September 27, 2026 — STEMBeginsHere, a youth-led Arizona nonprofit, wrapped its inaugural Free Hardware Bootcamp this weekend at Maricopa Institute of Technology, graduating 18 students in grades 6 through 12 from Gilbert, Chandler, and Phoenix-area schools including Kings Ridge, Kings Ridge Preparatory Academy, and Western Valley Middle School.

Over two Saturday sessions, students with no prior experience wired real circuits on breadboards, wrote code that ran on physical microcontrollers, and designed and built original hardware projects from scratch — everything from motion-triggered alarms to two-player reaction games. On the final day, families joined for a live showcase where students demonstrated their projects and explained how they worked, followed by a certificate ceremony recognizing every participant.

For many families, the program filled a real gap. Hands-on hardware engineering — kits, instructors, and curriculum — is rarely available through public schools, and private STEM camps offering similar instruction often cost hundreds of dollars. By bringing the program directly to a public school campus at no cost, STEMBeginsHere removed both barriers at once.

“Most kids never get a chance to touch the hardware side of computing before they've already decided what they're good at,” said Ronith Balusani, Founder of STEMBeginsHere. “We wanted to change that — to put a breadboard in front of a student before anyone tells them whether they're a ‘STEM kid’ or not.”

Parents in attendance praised the program's impact, with several noting how quickly their children moved from hesitation to confidently explaining their own working circuits to a room of adults.

STEMBeginsHere is planning additional sessions and is seeking school and community partners for its next cohort.

About STEMBeginsHere

STEMBeginsHere is an Arizona nonprofit corporation providing free, hands-on hardware engineering education to students in grades 6-12. Learn more at stembeginshere.org.
