What we provide

So you can walk in ready.

  • ✓A short orientation on the curriculum, the kits, and how we run a room.
  • ✓Tips from our instructors on common wiring mistakes and how to nudge students past them.
  • ✓All the hardware. You never need to bring or buy anything.
  • ✓A lead instructor in every session, so you are never on your own.
  • ✓A record of your hours for school service requirements and resumes.

What we ask

A few simple expectations.

  • ✓Guide, don't solve. A circuit that doesn't work yet is the lesson.
  • ✓Show up for the workshop days you commit to. Schools plan around us.
  • ✓Complete any background check the host school or district requires for adults working with students.
  • ✓Students under 18 are welcome as mentors with a parent or guardian's permission.
